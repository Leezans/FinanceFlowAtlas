# Asia, Africa, Middle East and Pacific: round 4

Checked 2026-11-04. 53 unique new economy/territory records, no overlap with the 166-economy prior-context snapshot (154 prior-round economies plus 12 baseline registry leads). These are discovery records, NOT 53 usable equity series. All remain not_certified; no new production adapter or display/redistribution permission is claimed.

## Results
- 30 national/territory candidates. BEAC publishes separate country workbooks for six CEMAC members, not a CEMAC total assigned to each member.
- 1 regional republisher candidate (SPC/Nauru), not a verified national download.
- 10 territorial-scope records explaining US/Australian/French aggregates; no separate equity data and no allocation of national totals.
- 1 historically documented gap (Wallis and Futuna, official 2021 note, not a permanent absence claim).
- 11 discovery gaps or agency/metadata-only leads. Missing sources have blank URLs; no fictitious endpoint was constructed.

## Stronger tests and blockers
- Chad, Central African Republic and Cameroon: anonymous direct HTTP200 XLSX responses, valid ZIP/XML and portfolio/equity labels. Numeric row mapping, stocks/income/flow separation and whether labels have populated observations remain unverified. No raw values retained.
- Kiribati: official quarterly workbook index opened; web reader could not parse binary, independent direct download returned403. This is a blocker, not proof an account is required, and no bypass was attempted.
- Niue: current fiscal2024/25 PDF opened,8pages. No equity text match, portfolio income is not portfolio transactions. Official revisions/gap caution plus apparent HTML reconciliation issues prohibit ingesting without validation.
- FSM: official external-sector HTML opened; repeated values/labels across differently titled tables warrant validation.
- BEA territory methodology indexed but direct PDF fetch timed out. ABS2018 methodology explicitly includes four listed territories; this is dated methodology, with Norfolk measurement caveat. French methodology2023 opened.

## Scope and rights
Statistical areas are explicit in each row. Country publication coverage does not establish a perfect economic perimeter; unresolved territories stay unresolved. Western Sahara UK counterparty aggregation is not a national source or a sovereignty inference. No UK totals assigned to overseas territories; Tokelau not automatically assigned New Zealand; independent FSM/Palau/Marshall Islands not assigned US.

Free, no-account access is the preference, not an assumption. Most candidates were search-indexed rather than downloaded; access_status states this. Automation and public-display rights remain unknown. No raw source datasets or numeric excerpts redistributed. Frequency is unknown unless evidence supports it; publication frequency is not observation frequency.

This regional round does not claim exhaustive ISO coverage: polar areas such as Antarctica and Bouvet were not researched as national reporting economies. Remaining universe gaps must remain visible in the master inventory.
